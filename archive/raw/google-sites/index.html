Welcome to PandAI



Welcome this is PandAI or the AI library which is currently inbuilt into the Panda3D 1.7.0 build. 

If this is your first time here then you should probably check out What is PandAI ?

This site has been created by the creators of PandAI to provide you with documentation, examples and tutorials on how to get started using this awesome AI library.

New to PandAI ? I would suggest looking at the Getting Started documentation.

Just want to jump in and use PandAI ? I would suggest getting right to it at the Download page.
